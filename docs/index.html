Validity

RN-first MVP

We are starting with registered nurses at travel nursing agencies, targeting California, Texas, Florida, New York, and Illinois.

RNs prove the verification loop. NPs and PAs prove the revenue model. Physicians follow commercial validation.

Implementation progress

The synthetic testnet validator evaluates fictional RN cases and preserves completed results across restarts, scores miner responses, and submits chain weights. Real-source coverage and agency workflows follow.

Miners and validators communicate over plain HTTP with hotkey-signed requests and responses. This connection uses no TLS, HTTPS, or certificates.
